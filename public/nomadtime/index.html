01 / Turn it sideways

See what the same hour feels like everywhere.

Morning in one place, evening in another. Landscape mode lines up your cities with colors for daylight, dusk, and night. Swipe through the hours to find a moment that works for everyone.

02 / Bring your routine

Where would your workday fit?
When would you sleep?

Save a range as Work, Sleep, Dance, or anything you like. Compare several at once, or switch one off to try a different plan.

Sleep from 10:30 PM to 6:00 AM crosses midnight naturally. The range stays connected as you compare it in other time zones.

03 / Make it personal

Sometimes a city is a person.

Name a clock for the person you’re thinking of. “Maya in Tokyo” makes it easier to answer the real question: is this a good time to call?

Open the tile, enter a Custom name, and tap Save name. The clock keeps the place’s time zone.

See the naming steps ↗

04 / Keep a little of every place

A map with your memories in it.

Drop a pin and keep the details you don’t want to lose: a sunset walk, directions to a favorite spot, or a note for next time.

Add your own photos and color labels. A linked clock lets you come back to the same notes from your world clock, too.

Made for possibility

You don’t have to be
on a trip to dream of one.

NomadTime began with a simple wish: to make the world feel more accessible. To imagine a life somewhere new, keep in touch across time zones, and remember the places that changed you.

Whether you’re coordinating five friends or wondering when you’d sleep in Tokyo, start by seeing your day there.
